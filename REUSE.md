modify, or incorporate the materials into another project.

not presented here as evidence of a patent grant. Its scope relative to each

For reuse of code, model assets, or published figures, contact the repository

source; it does not replace a reuse permission.

Third-party dependencies remain subject to their own licenses. Private sensor
recordings and participant documents are not distributed by this repository.
